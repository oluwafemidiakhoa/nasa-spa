What makes the 2026 build worth opening
Not another NASA dashboard. A mission you can interrogate.

The experience is organized around mission questions rather than a menu of unrelated apps. The user should be able to see a change, understand its consequence, and ask the system to explain the mission logic behind it.

01 · ExploreFly the Earth–Moon–Mars corridor in an interactive 3D mission view.
02 · ChangeAdjust mission parameters and see how orbital and trajectory context responds.
03 · UnderstandWalk through mission phases using a guided Story Mode instead of reading static documentation.
04 · Ask WhyUse the mission advisor to interrogate assumptions and explain what the mission state means.
