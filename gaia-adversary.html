Interpretation

Replay accept: the retained records reproduce the supplied report. This does not authenticate the records.

Source match: source bytes match the separately supplied digest. The selected site, threshold and report are not bound by this check.

Packet match: the complete proof bytes match the separately supplied digest. Neither digest match proves the origin or truth of the original bytes.

A mismatch is a discrepancy requiring investigation, not proof of fraud. Coverage, actual hazards, safety and trusted chronology remain unverified.
