Lock a prediction before testing it against later observations.

Specify a probability for a threshold of distinct matching EONET event IDs. Gaia retrieves a prior window, freezes a persistence baseline and creates a file-based trial record. Evaluate the fixed claim only after the target window has ended.

1. Define the trial

Baseline: seven complete UTC dates before the issue day. Target: seven complete UTC dates starting the next UTC day. You supply the probability; Gaia is not a trained forecasting model.
