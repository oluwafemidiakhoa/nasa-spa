Decision chain
HUMAN-READABLE
1
Mission phase changes what matters.

Lunar and Earth-proximal phases weight near-Earth geomagnetic conditions more heavily. Mars cruise phases weight broad solar-event indicators more heavily because Earth-local measurements are not the spacecraft's local plasma environment.

2
Live measurements add or remove pressure.

Kp, solar-wind speed, Bz, recent CMEs, and flare class contribute only when available. Missing feeds reduce confidence instead of being replaced with invented values.

3
The decision remains inspectable.

The prototype exposes every factor, its contribution, source availability, and whether a value is live or simulated.

4
A human sees the recommendation, not a black box.

GO, WATCH, HOLD, or VERIFY is a transparent prototype decision aid—not autonomous flight software.

Source health & provenance
0 / 5 LIVE

NASA DONKI provides recent CME and flare event context. NOAA SWPC provides near-Earth Kp, solar-wind plasma, and magnetic-field measurements. These feeds do not directly measure conditions at a spacecraft en route to Mars; the phase model explicitly reduces the weight of Earth-local measurements for deep-space phases.
