02 · Test the theory

Detection is not the discovery. The mismatch is.

Scientific value appears when an observation changes the weight of competing explanations. This is the reasoning loop the lab is designed to make visible.

1 · EXPECTED

Many very small bodies

Some formation models predict more tiny TNOs than the survey finds.

2 · OBSERVED

Fewer at the faint end

The measured ultra-faint population is shallower than some expectations.

3 · CONFLICT

Model pressure point

The population count does not simply continue the expected rise.

4 · ALTERNATIVES

Reweight explanations

Formation efficiency, collisional history, and surface evolution become competing explanations to test.

5 · NEXT TEST

Observe more faint TNOs

Expand wavelength coverage and completeness-controlled samples to discriminate among explanations.

What surprised scientists?

Two independent clues point backward in time.

Population: fewer very small objects than some models expected.

Surface colors: the smallest bodies continue color relationships seen in larger population members rather than obviously diverging after extensive collisional processing.

The responsible conclusion is not “the old model is wrong.” It is: these observations constrain which formation and evolution histories remain plausible.
