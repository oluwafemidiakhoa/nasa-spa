Observed result

27 newly discovered ultra-faint TNOs

NASA reports that coordinated Hubble visible-light observations and Webb infrared observations characterized tiny bodies beyond Neptune. The 2026 luminosity-function study reports a 40% detection threshold of F150W2 = 28.8 mag over 0.05 deg², using shift-and-stack plus a machine-learning network to reject false positives.

Interactive 03 · Scientific reasoning

Expectation vs. observation

Before the observations

Collision expectation
Very small TNOs were expected to have experienced many collisions that could change their surfaces.
Population expectation
Some formation models predicted more very small bodies.

What Hubble + Webb found

Surface colors persist
Small bodies follow color relationships seen among larger members of their populations.
Fewer very small bodies
NASA reports fewer tiny TNOs than expected from some planet-formation models.
Similar distributions
The hot and cold discovery subsamples are consistent with the same power-law slope within uncertainty.
